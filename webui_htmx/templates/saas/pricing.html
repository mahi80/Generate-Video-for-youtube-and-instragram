Questions

What counts as a video?

One finished video that you can download. Writing scripts, listening to voices and editing the script are free and unlimited.

Do failed videos use my allowance?

No. If something goes wrong while making a video, it doesn't count.

When does my allowance reset?

On the 1st of every month. Unused videos don't carry over.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. Footage comes from stock libraries that allow commercial use. Always watch your video before publishing it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly unless you choose yearly, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Indonesian, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Chinese, each with a native voice.
